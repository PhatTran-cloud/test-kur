# Evidence-based gates
Existing explicit authorization persists. Do not ask again merely because a gate exists. Record real evidence and invalidate only affected approvals on material change.

research_lock: sources/uncertainty; script_lock: visual hook/question/payoff/platform context; art_lock: original system/behavioral assets/styleframe coverage; animatic_lock: recorded VO or explicitly unnarrated authored soundtrack/readable state/rhythm; hero_lock: demanding pilot; sound_lock: deliberate sound choice and listening review (off valid); final_qa: encoded visual/motion/continuity/audio/performance.

Each review records status/reviewer/scope/evidence/observations/timestamp/blocking issues and binds current content hashes. Evidence exists and was inspected. No fabricated approval or self-score.
