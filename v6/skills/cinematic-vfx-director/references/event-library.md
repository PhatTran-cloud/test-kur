# Cinematic Event Library

Use this as a routing map, not a preset menu. Each event must adapt to material, scale, environment, camera and story.

## Impact

Punch/kick, body slam, projectile hit, melee strike, vehicle collision, metal impact, concrete impact, glass impact, object drop, crash.

Common channels: contact flash/chip, deformation, ejecta, dust/sparks, recoil, secondary vibration, camera impulse, residue.

## Heavy mass

Robot landing, creature stomp, vehicle touchdown, machinery engagement, giant door slam, structural drop.

Common channels: compression, low-frequency vibration, ground response, dust/debris, loose-object reaction, puddle ripple, camera mass response, mechanical settle.

## Pyro / combustion

Ignition, fire burst, fuel explosion, gas explosion, engine fire, electrical fire.

Common channels: flash, hot core, flame body, pressure, smoke, embers, local light, heat distortion, soot/scorch, residue.

## Destruction

Wall breach, building collapse, bridge failure, glass wall break, vehicle breakup, rock fracture.

Common channels: structural failure order, primary chunks, secondary debris, dust body, impact chains, occlusion, settling, changed silhouette/environment.

## Vehicle / speed

Race pass, drift, braking, launch, aerial flyby, train pass, boat pass.

Common channels: wake, tire smoke/dust/spray, debris/leaf displacement, suspension, reflections, near-camera streaks, buffeting, trailing atmosphere.

## Fluid

Splash, wave hit, waterfall impact, underwater burst, rain interaction, mud spray.

Common channels: sheet/crown, droplets, mist, ripples/waves, wetness, reflection/roughness change, gravity, drag, camera droplets if hit.

## Weather / atmosphere

Rain, snow, sandstorm, fog, ash, embers, dust storm, wind gust.

Common channels: coherent wind field, accumulation/deposition, visibility/contrast change, surface response, cloth/foliage coupling, depth/parallax.

## Electrical

Lightning, arc flash, damaged transformer, electrical discharge, energy overload.

Common channels: branching discharge, light flash, sparks/hot fragments, smoke, flicker, temporary power response, camera exposure response.

## Sci-fi / energy

Portal, shield hit, plasma bolt, laser impact, tractor field, holographic malfunction, reactor event.

Common channels: charge buildup, field geometry, emissive/refraction behavior, environmental force response, distortion, local light, residue/decay according to fictional rules.

## Magic / fantasy

Aura, rune activation, spell projectile, elemental burst, teleportation, summoning.

Common channels: anticipation/charge, coherent motif, field motion, environment reaction, illumination, particulate/volumetric support, residue. Keep internal magical rules consistent.

## Environmental micro-VFX

Breath condensation, dust motes, steam vent, dripping water, sweat mist, engine heat, tiny sparks, drifting ash, pollen, leaves.

Use sparingly to make the world alive; do not compete with hero action.

## Transition-capable VFX

Smoke/dust fill, flash/exposure, sparks/trails, rain/snow, spray, leaves/debris, volumetric darkness, energy ribbons, heat/refraction, glass/specular breakup.

A transition-capable effect must preserve or deliberately transform motion vector, density, phase, screen location, brightness, blur, depth and camera momentum.
