# Animal and creature system

## Species before style

Classify the subject before choosing geometry. A generic quadruped silhouette is not enough for a dog, cat, horse, deer, bear or big cat.

Inspect:

- back/spine curvature;
- chest depth and ribcage volume;
- pelvis size and angle;
- shoulder/scapular region;
- forelimb and hindlimb segment lengths;
- elbow/knee/hock orientation;
- paw/hoof proportions and contact;
- neck length and insertion;
- muzzle/skull proportions;
- ear placement;
- tail root and taper;
- characteristic stance and gait.

## Representation policy

High-fidelity target -> prefer an authored/rigged asset compatible with Three.js.

Stylized/procedural target -> procedural mesh is acceptable if the silhouette and articulation remain species-aware.

## Creature design

For fictional creatures, name the anatomical analogues used for each region. Preserve a coherent load path and joint system. Deliberate fantasy deviations are allowed; accidental contradictions are not.

## Motion

Gait must reflect morphology. Avoid moving all legs with the same phase. The torso, head, tail, ears and soft tissues receive delayed or coupled motion according to the action.
