# Storyboard Conformance Gate

Conformance is independent from beauty and runtime correctness.

## Mandatory checks

1. Identity matches the canonical asset brief.
2. Recognition traits are present at the intended viewing scale.
3. Required parts remain separate and named.
4. Required anchors/sockets exist and are testable.
5. Variant/state appearance follows the storyboard cause.
6. Dimensions/scale are compatible with the declared scene use.
7. Surface regions follow material/research logic.
8. Texture scale/direction is tied to construction, growth, wear, contact or environment.
9. Closest-camera inspection does not expose placeholder craft.
10. The asset remains independently editable and is not a flattened scene plate.

Status: PASS / FAIL / PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED.
PARTIAL and UNVERIFIED never promote to FINAL PASS.
