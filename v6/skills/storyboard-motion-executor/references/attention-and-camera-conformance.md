# Attention and camera conformance

## Attention hierarchy
Treat storyboard priority as a budget.

Hero should normally dominate through some combination of:
- contrast;
- motion;
- focus;
- size;
- occlusion/reveal;
- color/light;
- screen position;
- sound support.

Secondary elements may move richly without exceeding the hero's narrative salience.

Suppress decorative VFX/motion when it competes with comprehension.

## Camera Information Gate
For each camera move/hold, record:
1. what the viewer knows before;
2. what becomes visible/understandable;
3. what action or question motivates the move;
4. where the eye should land;
5. why moving is better than holding, or vice versa.

Bad reason:
"The star burns fuel faster."

Better reason:
"Hold both stars in one stable comparison frame so the viewer can perceive divergent fuel/lifetime state without camera motion becoming the change."

Camera path quality is subordinate to information clarity.
