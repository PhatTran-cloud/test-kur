# Motion/VFX conformance review

Status per gate: PASS / FAIL / PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED.

## Gates
1. Canonical IDs preserved.
2. All visible objects have justified temporal state.
3. Active/reactive motion has explicit cause.
4. Physical response fits mass/material/constraint.
5. Attention hierarchy preserved.
6. Camera execution preserves information/reveal intent.
7. Focus and parallax remain coherent.
8. Transition preserves the declared invariant or intentional discontinuity.
9. Persistent object/VFX state does not reset arbitrarily.
10. VFX expansion remains subordinate to story purpose.
11. Seek-safe behavior verified at non-sequential timestamps.
12. Endpoint states inspected.
13. Final encoded frames inspected.
14. Technical success is not substituted for visual approval.

PARTIAL and UNVERIFIED never promote to FINAL PASS.
