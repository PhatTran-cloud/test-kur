# Output ownership

Existing `output/<slug>/` film files are historical/generated production. Preserve them unless explicitly asked. New work belongs in `output/<slug>/v6/`: initialize with `v6_packet.py`, follow root rules and the v6 contract, then complete typed packets and real review evidence.

No automatic migration of old chapters/shots into approved v6 scenes. Output is not the source of shared production rules; those live in root and `v6/`. Encoded previews and evidence stay with the packet they validate.
